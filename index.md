# Privacy Policy — Pit Wall Manager

**Last updated: 23 October 2026**

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Pit Wall Manager handles information when you use the game.

Pit Wall Manager is developed and published by an individual developer. This policy describes the game's intended data-handling practices and should be read alongside the information provided in its Google Play Store listing.

## 2. Information We Collect

Pit Wall Manager is designed around gameplay and does not require users to create an account.

Gameplay information may be stored locally on your device to support the game's functionality, such as retaining game progress or settings, where those features are implemented.

The developer does not intentionally collect personal information directly through an account-registration system, as the game does not require accounts.

**Third-party services:** The game uses Google Play services. Depending on the specific services and features integrated into the game, Google may process information under its own privacy policies. Please consult Google's privacy policy for further information.

The exact information processed by Google Play services depends on the integrations enabled in the released version of the game.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information stored by the game is used to provide its gameplay functionality, including maintaining gameplay progress or settings where applicable.

Information processed by Google Play services may be used to provide the relevant Google Play functionality. The applicable purposes depend on the services integrated into the game.

## 4. Sharing of Information

The developer does not intentionally send gameplay information to other parties unless this is necessary for a feature or service disclosed in this policy.

Google Play services may process information in connection with their functionality. Any additional data sharing through third-party libraries or services must be assessed and disclosed before the game is published.

## 5. Data Storage, Retention and Deletion

Gameplay information stored locally remains on the device until it is deleted by the game, removed through an available game feature, or removed through device or application data-management functions, as applicable.

The developer does not operate a user-account system for Pit Wall Manager.

Any information processed or retained by Google Play services is subject to the relevant service's data-handling and retention practices.

## 6. Data Security

The developer aims to minimise the information handled by the game and avoid collecting information that is not necessary for its functionality.

The security of information processed by third-party services is subject to the relevant provider's security practices. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Pit Wall Manager is intended for a general audience. The developer does not intentionally collect personal information directly from children through an account-registration system.

The game's target age groups and any third-party SDKs must be assessed against the applicable Google Play Families requirements before release.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the game's features, integrations or data-handling practices change. The latest version will be published at the privacy policy URL provided in the game's Google Play Store listing.

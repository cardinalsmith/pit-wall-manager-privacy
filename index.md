# Privacy Policy — Pit Wall Manager

**Last updated: 23 October 2026**

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Pit Wall Manager handles information when you use the game.

Pit Wall Manager is developed and published by an individual developer and is intended for users aged 13 and older.

## 2. Information Collection

Pit Wall Manager does not require users to create an account.

The game is designed to store gameplay information, such as game progress and saved-game data, locally on your device to support its functionality.

The game does not currently integrate Google Play services.

The developer does not intentionally collect personal information directly from users through an account-registration system. This statement does not rule out information processed automatically by the Android operating system or any third-party libraries included in the app.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Gameplay information stored locally is used to support the game's functionality, including saving and loading game progress where applicable.

The developer does not operate an online account system or a game server for Pit Wall Manager.

## 4. Sharing of Information

The developer does not intentionally transmit locally stored gameplay information to the developer or other parties.

This statement applies to the game's intended local gameplay functionality. It does not cover any data transmission that may occur through third-party libraries, Android system features, device backups, or other integrations unless these have been separately verified.

## 5. Data Storage, Retention and Deletion

Gameplay save data is stored locally on the device.

Users may be able to remove this information by using any relevant in-game reset or deletion functionality, or by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game. The precise behaviour depends on the app's implementation and Android's data-management features.

The developer does not maintain a central database of player accounts or gameplay saves.

## 6. Data Security

Pit Wall Manager is designed to keep gameplay information on the user's device rather than transmit it to a game server.

The security of locally stored information depends on the app's implementation and the security of the device on which it is installed.

## 7. Children's Privacy and Intended Audience

Pit Wall Manager is registered for age groups 13 and older.

The game is not intended to be specifically directed at children under 13. The developer does not intentionally collect personal information directly from users through account registration.

The app's target audience, content, and any included third-party libraries must comply with the applicable Google Play policies.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the game's functionality or data-handling practices change.

The latest version will be available at the public privacy policy URL provided in the Google Play Store listing.
